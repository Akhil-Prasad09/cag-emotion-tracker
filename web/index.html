Emotion Tracker

Real-time facial emotion recognition. Everything runs in your browser: your camera feed and photos never leave this device. A CNN embeds each face and the emotion comes from a cache of 7 prototype embeddings. It scores 70.6% on the FER-2013 test set; in-browser face detection frames faces a little differently, so expect a few points less here.
